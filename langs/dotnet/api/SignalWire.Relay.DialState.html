Remarks

The RELAY calling.call.dial event carries a dial_state of dialing, answered, or failed (see HandleDialEvent(Event, Dictionary<string, object>) and DialStateDialing .. DialStateFailed). It is the dial-outcome vocabulary and is deliberately distinct from CallState (the per-call lifecycle) — a dial that resolves answered transitions the winning Call to Answered, but the two fields are separate on the wire.

Both answered and failed are terminal dial outcomes (the dial is resolved); dialing is in-progress. Parse via TryParse(string?, out DialState), which tolerates an unknown server value.
