using signalwire::utils::url_validator::ResolverFn
Initial value:
std::function<std::optional<std::vector<std::string>>(const std::string&)>

Pluggable resolver.

Tests inject a callable to keep the suite hermetic; production resolves via getaddrinfo. Returns nullopt on resolution failure.

The signature: function(hostname) -> optional<vector<string>> of IP-string addresses.
